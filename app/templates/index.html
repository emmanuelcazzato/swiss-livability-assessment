Swiss Livability Assessment

Experience the intersection of data and perception. Our fuzzy logic system translates complex environmental metrics into human-centric livability scores.

What We Assess

  • Acoustic Comfort Based on WHO 2018 noise guidelines
  • Daylight Quality Based on EN 17037 standards
  • View Quality Sky visibility and greenery exposure
  • Location Convenience Accessibility to amenities

How It Works

1
Input Environmental measurements (noise, daylight, views, POIs)
2
Fuzzification Convert numbers to linguistic terms (e.g., "Quiet", "Bright")
3
Inference Apply 15 expert-defined fuzzy rules
4
Output Fuzzy Livability Index (0-100) with linguistic label

Livability Levels

Excellent 65-100

Quiet environment, high daylight, good views

Good 45-75

Balanced environmental quality

Fair 25-55

Moderate quality, some improvements needed

Poor 0-35

Significant environmental challenges

About This Project

This is a proof-of-concept implementation of the Swiss Residential Perceived Livability Assessment Framework. The system processes data from the Swiss Dwellings v3.0.0 dataset and uses a Mamdani Fuzzy Inference System to compute livability scores that align with human perception.
